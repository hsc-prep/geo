How the suite fits together

  1. Pace yourself with the 6-Week Planner — a week-by-week checklist counting down to the HSC exam.
  2. Rate how you're feeling below and let the hub recommend where to start.
  3. Drill recall with the Flashcards page (quick, covers every topic including Buddhism and Judaism) or the full Multiple Choice bank (hundreds of real and practice questions, with a mastery/archive system).
  4. Check yourself with the 4-question Quick Quiz — a fast gut-check drawn from all 19 real past HSC Q11 questions, randomised each time.
  5. Practise writing with real past Question 11s — one at a time, on a 10-minute timer, with NESA's own marking criteria, sample answer, and an extended model answer. Score yourself 5/5 and it archives.
  6. Buddhism and Judaism depth studies each have their own study pack — content bank, flashcards, a named-evidence bank and real HSC questions with marking criteria, plus a Q11-style practice-writing process with a timer and self-scoring.
  7. Get feedback on your own answers using the "Copy AI-marking prompt" button in the Q11 tool, then paste into Copilot or another AI tool your school provides.
